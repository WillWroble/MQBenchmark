The board is a grid of characters, one row per line:
A, B, C, D  clones
.  floor
#  wall
_  void
G  goal
~  smooth floor
S  slider, on smooth floor
T  teleporter
t  exit-only teleporter
O  drain
A clone or slider hides the square under it; any square under one that isn't plain floor or smooth floor is listed below the board. Rows are numbered from 1 at the top and columns from 1 at the left.
