Mirror Quest

Moves 0Minimum –

Rules

  • Every move sends all clones one step the same way.
  • A clone that hits a wall stays put.
  • If anything would fall into the void, nothing moves.
  • If two things would land on one square, nothing moves.
  • Crates slide on ice, teleporters and drains, never on stone.
  • A clone on a drain is stuck until another pushes it.
  • Teleporters send you to their partner; a dim one is exit only.
  • Win with every clone on a gold ring.

←↑↓→ or WASD or swipe to move, Z to undo, R to reset.
